# Kairos

**Status:** Early concept / initial scaffold
**Stage:** Exploration
**When:** July 2026
**Code:** Private for now

## The idea

Kairos is meant to be a creative platform for **discovering, sharing and creatively remixing digital artwork**.

The visual direction I'm going for is editorial and Y3K-inspired.

None of that is built yet. This page describes where I want it to go, not what exists.

## What actually exists

- A Next.js scaffold (one commit)
- A first hero section on the landing page: **"CREATE. REMIX. INSPIRE."**

That's all. No accounts, uploads, feed or remixing logic.

## Current focus

Working out the product before writing more code: what problem it solves for artists, what "remix" actually means in practice (credit, versions, permissions), and what the first usable version looks like.

## Next

Once the concept is clearer, build the core loop first: upload a piece, remix it, see the chain. Then worry about the rest.
